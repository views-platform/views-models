          mean has an equivalent here. If the work rests on one of the other two, the two
          deliveries are not comparable and it is better to know before they are placed side by
          side.

          data error?} At 113,395 fatalities against a next-largest of 5,395 it will dominate any
          squared-error metric computed over the final origin, and the answer changes how that
          origin should be handled.
\end{enumerate}
